How we work

Six steps, the same every time

You always know what happens next and who is doing it.

  1. 01

    Brief and site visit

    We walk the space with you and note seat count, teams, move-in date and budget.

  2. 02

    Layout

    We place desks, cabins, meeting rooms and storage on your floor plan, planned around how your people work.

  3. 03

    3D view

    You see your office furnished before anything is ordered, and we revise it with you.

  4. 04

    Quotes from partner factories

    We compare factories side by side on price, finish, lead time and warranty, so you can choose with the facts in front of you.

  5. 05

    Delivery and installation

    The factory makes and fits the furniture. We coordinate dates, check quality and keep you updated.

  6. 06

    Handover

    We walk the floor with you, close the snag list and explain the warranty in writing.

Questions

Before you start

Is the layout really free?

Yes, for qualified enquiries where we know the seat count, move-in date and budget range.

Do you only work in Pune?

We focus on Hinjewadi, Kharadi and Magarpatta, and take projects elsewhere in Pune.

How big a project do you take on?

From about 10 seats to full floors. Our packages run from Starter, for 10 to 25 seats, to Executive, for 50 seats and up.

Can I use my own furniture supplier?

Yes. We can still plan the layout, and include your supplier's quote alongside our partner factories.
