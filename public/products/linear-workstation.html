Linear workstation, 4 seats

A straight run of four desks on a shared frame. The simplest way to seat a team, and easy to extend in pairs.

  • Shared frame, so runs can be extended two seats at a time
  • Low divider screen between facing desks
  • Cable tray under the top with a flip-up access flap
  • Levelling feet for uneven floors

Questions

Before you order

Can the size or finish be changed?

Usually, yes. Partner factories can adjust sizes and finishes within their range. Tell us what you need and we confirm it in the quote.

How long does delivery take?

Lead time depends on the factory, the finish and the quantity. We give you a confirmed date with the quote.

Who installs it?

The factory or its installation partner fits it on site, and we coordinate the visit and the snag list.

What warranty do I get?

The warranty is the factory's. We share its terms in writing with the quote and help with any service request.

Can I see it before ordering?

Yes. We can show you a sample, take you to the factory or showroom, or send a 3D view of it in your layout.
